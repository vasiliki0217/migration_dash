When you open it you'll see:

-A world map (big, on the left)
-Two bar charts on the right — top receivers and top senders
-A year slider at the top
-Some number badges in the header (these update as you explore)

how do I  use it?

1. Move the year slider
Drag it left or right to change the year. Everything updates instantly. Start by sliding from 1990 to 2024 and watch the map get darker — global migration has grown a lot.

2. Look at the bar charts

-Top chart → countries that received the most migrants that year (blue)
-Bottom chart → countries that sent the most migrants that year (orange)

3. Click on a country
This is where it gets interesting. Click any country on the map and:

-The map switches to show where that country's migrants came from or went to
-Two buttons appear — Origins and Destinations — toggle between them
-A bottom panel slides open with two more charts:
	-Gender chart — male vs. female split for that country
	-Trend chart — migration over time, compared to the 4 nearest countries geographically. The dotted line = the year you selected.
	
4. Go back to global view
Hit "Clear selection" at the bottom of the map.
